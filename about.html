Who we are

About SafeByte

SafeByte was built by Group 5 for the CSS Team Project to help everyday people stay safe online.

Our mission

Cybercrime costs Americans billions of dollars every year, and most of it starts with a simple trick. Our mission is to explain online safety in plain language so that students, parents and grandparents can protect themselves.

After visiting SafeByte, we want you to turn on MFA, upgrade one password, and know how to report a scam.
