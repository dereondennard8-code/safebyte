Protect yourself

Everyday Safety Tips

Small habits, big protection. Work through these tips one at a time – each takes only a few minutes.

Build a strong password

  1. Make it at least 16 characters long.
  2. Use a random phrase, like purple-taco-rocket-lamp.
  3. Use a different password for every account.
  4. Let a password manager create and remember them.

Your safety checklist

Accounts

  • Turn on multifactor authentication (MFA) for email, banking and social media.
  • Use an authenticator app instead of text codes when possible.
  • Review which apps have access to your accounts.

Devices

  • Turn on automatic updates for your phone and computer.
  • Lock your phone with a PIN, fingerprint, or face ID.
  • Back up important files to the cloud or an external drive.

On the go

  • Avoid banking on public Wi-Fi, or use your phone’s hotspot.
  • Check for “https://” and the real web address before logging in.
  • Think twice before posting your location or school online.
