Know your enemy

Common Cyber Threats

Criminals use a handful of tricks over and over. Once you can name them, they are much easier to avoid.

Phishing: the #1 threat

Phishing is a message – email, text, or social media DM – that pretends to come from someone you trust, like your bank, school, or a delivery company. The goal is to get you to click a bad link, open an infected attachment, or type in your password.

Warning signs

  • Urgent language: “Your account will be closed today!”
  • Offers that are too good to be true
  • Misspelled sender addresses or web links
  • Requests for passwords, codes, or gift cards

Got a suspicious message?

  1. Stop. Don’t click links or reply.
  2. Check. Contact the company using a number you already trust.
  3. Report. Forward scam texts to 7726 (SPAM) and report email phishing to your provider.
  4. Delete. Remove the message.

Five common threats and how to defend against them
ThreatWhat it isRiskBest defense

MalwareHarmful software that spies on or damages your deviceHighKeep software updated; download only from official stores
